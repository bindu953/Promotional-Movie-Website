Synopsis

Raised with nothing in the shadows of Charlotte’s booming skyline, brothers Bryan Santos and Paul Chapman share one relentless ambition: to own the city they grew up in. Bound by loyalty and driven by survival the two forge an unstoppable partnership in the Queen City’s criminal underworld. Bryan known on the streets as “El Estratega,” operates from the shadows calculating every move three steps ahead, managing the money, and expanding their reach into legitimate fronts. Paul feared and respected as “Big Merch,” commands the ground using raw charisma street smarts, and sheer nerve to turn impossible odds into lucrative territory.

Together, El Estratega and Big Merch build a sprawling, criminal, empire that stretches from gritty neighborhood corners to Uptown’s highest penthouses. With wealth and influence, however comes unwanted attention. Their rapid ascent threatens The Enemy, a ruthless deeply entrenched rival kingpin determined to crush the upstart brothers and reclaim the city.

Just as Bryan and Paul prepare to solidify their reign, their empire begins to fracture from within. Shipments vanish, safe houses are hit, and federal investigators suddenly know their every move. The brothers realize the unthinkable: The Traitor, someone inside their tightest inner circle, trusted like family has quietly aligned with their deadliest rival.Paranoia spreads like wildfire. With law enforcement closing in, enemies circling for the kill and their own crew.

Fracturing, Bryan’s cold logic and Paul’s volatile instincts collide. Forced into a corner where trust is a fatal weakness, the brothers must make an impossible choice: abandon the empire they bled to build, protect the family they swore to keep safe, or wage an all-out war for revenge. In a city where power is everything, Bryan and Paul are about to prove that while loyalty made them kings, betrayal makes them lethal.
