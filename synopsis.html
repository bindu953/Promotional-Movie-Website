Synopsis

A detective investigates a mysterious crime and uncovers secrets that change everything. For years, the underground syndicate operated in the shadows, ruled with an iron fist by two legendary kingpins who controlled every line of logic in the city. Now, the underworld invites you to experience Brothers in Crime, an explosive, high octane thriller about power, betrayal, and absolute territorial dominance.

In his gripping, career defining performance, Bryan Santos stars as El Estratega, a calculated mastermind who built a multimillion dollar empire on unbreakable architecture, absolute obedience, and expecting clean commits from every operative under his command.

Co-starring alongside him is the unshakeable Paul Chapman, who embodies his role as Big Merch, who runs the perimeter defense, handles the heavy enforcement, and ensures that no loose ends or errors ever survive the night.

When El Estratega and Big Merch are forced into an uneasy alliance to pull off the ultimate digital heist, immediate sparks fly as their conflicting methods threaten to bring down their entire operation. The stakes reach a boiling point when a rival faction tries to infiltrate their secure repository, forcing the two kingpins to decide whether they can trust each other... or if they'll go down in a blaze of ''syntax'' flames before grading anyone's code.
